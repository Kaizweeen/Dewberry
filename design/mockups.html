2. Draw

Draw freehand on the paper in any color. The square picks how light and how strong the color is, the strip picks the hue, and the chips are the colors already used in this drawing, for getting an exact color back. One line meter drains as you draw, whatever the color. The eraser removes a whole line and gives its length back. Shown mid-drawing. The meter is worked out from the lines on the paper. The meter's size changes every day.
