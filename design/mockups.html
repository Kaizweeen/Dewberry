Grout screen mockups

Five screens for the daily loop, each at desktop size (1440 x 1024, laid out like your Figma frames) and at phone size (390 x 760). The sky, grass, bush, shadow, pets, nameplate, home button, and waffle panel are your art, exported from Figma and untouched. Everything else is new and needs your approval.

The drawings and player names here are samples made for this page. They are not real entries. "Grout" is still a working title.
